## Research Transparency

The research includes recorded real-LLM evaluations using synthetic scenarios and simulated tool operations. Deterministic toy-model demonstrations are distinguished from actual language-model observations.

The adversarial security benchmark underwent single-reviewer human label validation. The findings remain exploratory and do not establish production-level security effectiveness or general model vulnerability rates.

Research findings, experimental limitations, and contributor responsibilities should be reported accurately.

Individual institutional affiliations do not imply official organizational sponsorship, endorsement, or participation.
